## Summary

The concept of Contextual Pretrained Experts (CPTEs) has been successfully published as a LinkedIn article, introducing the broader AI community to this novel approach for knowledge lifecycle management in AI systems.

The article explains how CPTEs:
- Mirror human knowledge decay and delegation patterns
- Integrate with the Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM) for situational awareness
- Enable AI systems to be both "wise" (through HRM) and "learned" (through CPTEs)
- Provide practical benefits for scalable AI architectures

This publication represents a key milestone in sharing innovations from the AI DNA Discovery project with the wider community.
